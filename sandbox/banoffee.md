# Banoffee Pie

A classic, highly rated banoffee pie from BBC Good Food.

## Ingredients
- 175g digestive biscuits, finely crushed
- 75g butter, melted
- 397g can caramel or dulce de leche
- 4 bananas, sliced
- 300ml double cream
- Dark chocolate, to garnish

## Method
1. Mix the biscuit crumbs with melted butter, press into a pie tin, and chill.
2. Bake the base until set and golden, then cool completely.
3. Spread half the caramel over the base, add the banana slices, and cover with the remaining caramel.
4. Chill the pie.
5. Whip the cream until thick, spoon it on top, and finish with grated dark chocolate.

## Notes
- Best served chilled.
- This version is simple, classic, and rated very highly on BBC Good Food.
